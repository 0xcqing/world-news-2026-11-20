# World News · Friday 20 November 2026

A single 1024×1024 poster sums up Friday, 20 November 2026, a day of widening conflict and diplomacy. Houthi strikes damaged the PIF Tower in Riyadh's King Abdullah Financial District and Saudi Arabia struck Sanaa, while Trump said he would not rush a decision on Iran as the IRGC rejected a war of attrition and Qatar kept mediating. Putin ruled out maritime ceasefire talks after three days of attacks on the Crimean Bridge, and Kushner said Moscow would accept European participation in talks and NATO-style guarantees for Ukraine. APEC closed in Shenzhen with a Xi-Putin-Trump peace declaration, ten thousand people protested in Tokyo against the Takaichi government, and oil rose on war risk, with WTI near 82 dollars and Brent near 93, while Comcast joined the bidding for Warner Bros. Discovery at 108 billion dollars.

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` copies `entry.png` into the output directory, then re-reads the copy to verify its PNG signature, byte size and dimensions.
